# DownKit name and logo

The DownKit **source code** is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE),
© 2026 aderimo. You may use, change and redistribute the code, as long as the
copyright notice and the license text stay with it.

The **name "DownKit"**, the **duck logo** (`branding/`, `src-tauri/icons/`) and the
"by aderimo" mark are not part of that license. They belong to aderimo and
identify the official releases published at
[github.com/Aderimo/downkit](https://github.com/Aderimo/downkit).

## What is fine without asking

- Sharing unmodified official releases (download pages, mirrors, package managers).
- Saying that your project is based on DownKit ("a fork of DownKit", "built on DownKit").
- Using the name and logo in articles, reviews, tutorials and videos about DownKit.

## What needs a different name and logo

- Publishing a modified version (a fork) as a program people download and install.
- Anything that could make people think a build is official or made by aderimo
  when it is not.

Forks are welcome: give yours its own name and icon, keep the MIT copyright
notice, and you are good to go.
